# Four films / director’s edit

These are directed relationships between independent reference photographs, not evidence of a shared collection, campaign or garment-development history. Five composed moments per canvas; ten looks per archive. All keyframes can be held indefinitely. Camera descriptions refer to two-dimensional photographic planes and source windows, never invented viewpoints or body motion.

## Artistic assessment

The architectural Streetwear passage is the most specific source-based cinematic idea. Activewear’s actual suspended pose provides the clearest physical release. RTW develops posture and scene density; Tailoring relies on editorial cutting and authentic light contrast rather than effects. Their vocabulary is intentionally different, but the strength of future campaign continuity still depends on owner-supplied photography. The present sources are independent references, not a custom-shot film. The owner’s artistic approval remains separate from functional verification.
