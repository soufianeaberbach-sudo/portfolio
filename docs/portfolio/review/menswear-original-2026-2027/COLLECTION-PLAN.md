## Merchandising and casting

Two tailoring looks plus eight distinct outerwear families: wool overcoat, trench, leather blouson, Harrington, puffer, parka, field jacket and shearling aviator. Streetwear nylon tracksuit and athletic tricot track jacket have different materials, proportions and use. Casual coordinated sets differ in garment family and construction. The final casual layering slot becomes a defined chore jacket.

A ★ marks one of the eight pilots. Full construction, casting, grooming, fabric, styling, footwear and three-view directions are in COLLECTION-PLAN.json. All forty casting identities are fictional adult models; visual distinctness will be reviewed, not inferred from descriptions.

## Pilot order and quality gates

Phase B produces all eight individual Front photographs before companion production. Review product identity, design, adult casting, anatomy, textile behavior, seams, full-length framing and white studio. A failed Front stays rejected or on hold; it does not authorize companion views.

Phase C generates true rear and campaign Editorial views from each accepted new Front, reviews continuity and records visible deviations. Native images are immutable; every accepted image is decoded, dimensioned, SHA256 checked, committed, pushed and remotely verified before the next accepted checkpoint. Exports use uniform scaling, with explicit resampling disclosure.

Phase D stops at eight pilot looks for owner artistic approval. Thirty-two concepts stay PLANNED. Owner approval, agent review, remote save, public integration, merge and deployment are separate states. No automatic publication.

## Technical limits

These are design-intent construction notes, not production tech packs. Hidden internals, physical fit, seam strength, shrinkage, weather protection, sweat transport and warmth remain untested. AI photographs are evaluated for visible plausibility only. Model continuity is a visual judgment, not biometric proof.

## Market research

Primary retail references were searched/opened on 23 October 2026. Cached page dates vary; stock and regional selections can change. Observed product families support commercial relevance, not proof of future trends. No 2027 market claim is made. Retail images, logos and campaign identities are not generation inputs.

- **R01** [Represent streetwear taxonomy](https://representclo.com/collections/shop): Separate tee, hoodie, sweatshirt, trouser, denim and knit families; current collection navigation includes 2026 editions. Retail category observation; does not prove a forecast or original design.
- **R02** [Represent denim](https://representclo.com/collections/denim): Retail describes slim, straight and baggy fit families plus cargo and carpenter jeans. Design decision: use relaxed proportion without adopting any branded garment or campaign.
- **R03** [COS coats and jackets](https://www.cos.com/en-gb/men/coats-and-jackets): Current category distinguishes leather, denim, Harrington, puffer, parka, trench, shearling and wool outerwear. Category evidence; regional/cache product selections vary.
