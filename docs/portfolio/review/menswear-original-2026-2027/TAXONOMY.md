The final category keeps **two tailoring products and eight outerwear families**. A long wool coat, belted trench, leather blouson, short Harrington, baffled insulated jacket, hooded parka, pocketed field jacket and shearling aviator differ in silhouette, closure, material and construction.

Cross-category distinctions are intentional: the Streetwear tracksuit uses crinkle nylon and wide fashion proportions; the Activewear track jacket uses stretch tricot and athletic articulation. The knit vest is an urban layered garment, while the athletic vest has mesh expansion and flat functional storage. Casual linen and twill sets have different garment constructions and seasonal materials. Repeating a colour or changing a face does not define a new product.
