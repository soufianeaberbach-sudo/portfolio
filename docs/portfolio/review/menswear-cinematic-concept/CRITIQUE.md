## Final static review

Inspected all twenty scene rests in each of six viewport grids, then native-size
openings, threshold, payoff, menu, archive, reader and inspection captures.
At 1440×900, the Streetwear threshold is the strongest frame: bright photographic
architecture is held by ink without recoloring the garment. Its new teal finale
changes silhouette and palette through a real look. The three-up Tailoring archive
is utility after desire, not a proposed hero lineup.

At 390×844 and 430×932, the complete hoodie, city stride, booth look and teal
outfit retain identity. The low Activewear pose stays complete and genuinely
contrasts with standing looks. The mobile typography above those sources is
intentional; provenance and controls stay outside the garment. The 768×1024 layout
uses the narrow composition instead of forcing desktop pairs onto a portrait screen.

## Actual motion review

Decoded the final continuous desktop and mobile browser recordings at **4 fps**:
90 desktop frames and 96 mobile frames. Reviewed the timestamped contact sheets
in full, including start, intermediate gate movement, held reveal, final look,
presentation interruption, direct Tailoring jump, reader entry, zoom/Fit and return.
No blank scene, phantom duplicate body, automatic Back turn or stranded return
clone was observed in those samples. This is sampled visual inspection, not a
frame-perfect certification at the original recording frame rate.

The gate deliberately passes across typography during the brief threshold
transition; both rest states have unobscured titles. Photographic plates stay
opaque and proportionate. It is a change of surrounding atmosphere, not false
camera travel through the person's body. Source windows do not fake a registered
match cut to an uncropped original.

## Remaining artistic reservations

1. Streetwear is the principal motion proposal. The other worlds have composed
   scene/keynote studies; their final timing and scroll cinematography remain
   unapproved, not secretly complete.
2. Activewear mobile reuses the earlier standing black look for its ending.
   Tailoring mobile scenes 2 and 3 repeat the same light-studio photo. These
   proposals need stronger differentiation before a full-world implementation.
3. Headline-left / photo-right establishment recurs across desktop worlds. The
   common vocabulary is coherent, but the owner must judge whether its range is
   strong enough for the intended luxury-stage standard. More motion alone would
   not fix a weak photographic edit.
4. Independent reference backgrounds cannot become a single real campaign without
   corresponding photography. Intact room boundaries are deliberate. New owner
   photography should provide consistent sets and authentic angles, not filters
   or generated relighting.
5. Stage-screen impact, physical Safari, actual presenter remotes and projection
   brightness are not proven by Chromium screenshots. 1800px-high candidates
   establish composition; they are not a final 4K image-delivery decision.

The concept is ready for a critical artistic decision, not declared creatively
approved or production-ready.
