Full paths, all fourteen source URLs, actual local dimensions, SHA-256 hashes,
modifications and available views are in [ASSETS.json](ASSETS.json). New files are
proportional 1800px-high downloads converted to WebP; no retouching, background
removal, relighting or generated content. Original camera dimensions are recorded
only where the actual source disclosed them, otherwise unknown rather than guessed.
The green-trouser and kneeling images use explicit empty-margin display windows;
the complete original remains in the reader/inspection. No destructive crop is saved.

Existing source credits in portfolio-editorial.ts do not record photographer
names. The proof links the actual Pexels pages and does not invent missing names.
Those files retain their original paths/content. Research alternatives rejected
for torso crops, weak primary framing or rear-view suitability remain /tmp only.

Fonts and GSAP are the repository's existing dependencies/fixtures, not a new
identity or framework. The offline review references the repository's original
font/source files; they are not duplicated into a new public asset library.
