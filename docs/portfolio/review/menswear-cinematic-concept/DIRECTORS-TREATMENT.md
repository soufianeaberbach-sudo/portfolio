# HELD / REVEALED

## A proposed director's treatment — not creative approval

**Luxury fashion drama × visual suspense × speaker-controlled keynote.**
The subject is presence, expressed through four different relationships to space.
A photograph is a room, not a transparent cutout. The screen behaves like an
editorial camera gate: it holds, reveals a relationship, makes one decisive cut,
and then lets the complete look command the frame.

Emotional arc: **curiosity → anticipation → surprise → admiration → discovery**.
The first full silhouette creates desire. A second photographic axis creates
expectation. A material change in the *surrounding stage* changes the reading of
the same preserved photography. The final held composition rewards attention.
The archive immediately releases the audience from the directed sequence.

### Alternatives rejected before implementation

A moving runway would repeat Womenswear. Fake projection/3D turns would falsify
flat photography. Literal theatrical curtains, spotlights and an opera-house UI
would illustrate the metaphor instead of presenting clothes. A ten-slide film
would confuse collection capacity with dramatic necessity.

The selected grammar uses complete photographic plates, one or two at a time;
their own architecture, pose, density and background create tension. No body
warping or simulated photographic relighting. Scale changes only to change
context or establish a relationship, never to arbitrarily rank look numbers.

## Shared cinematography

- **Perspective:** real two-dimensional photographic planes; no rotateY,
  perspective distortion, synthetic shadow, fake depth map or invented viewpoint.
- **Framing:** complete available silhouette in each primary rest, within safe
  typography/control margins. Specific empty source margins may be windowed;
  no body crop masquerades as the main look. Original is always available.
- **Lighting:** the real image lighting remains fixed. Bone/white/ink surrounding
  fields can change how a plate is read, but never its colors or luminance.
- **Type:** existing Archivo / Inter / JetBrains Mono. One short authored scene
  phrase supplies the compositional counterweight; a small world/scene marker
  orients. No decorative technical graphics or excessive garment metadata.
- **Depth:** photographic rooms relate through aligned edges, floor positions
  and directional gazes, not implied common floors or arbitrary human scaling.
- **Timing:** each rest is indefinite in Presentation Mode. Ordinary scroll can
  continuously approach/exchange frames; a reveal is approximately 0.5–0.75s for
  deliberate Next/Previous. Reduced motion cuts directly. No autoplay/audio.
- **Access:** world jump and archive are available before the first scene.
  Skipping a world never traverses its intermediate shots.

## Navigation and executive presentation

A small **Worlds** selector reveals four named destinations. Each action settles
any current transition and lands on the selected world's opening directly.
Previous/Next and five named scene marks support non-linear access within a world.
The archive is always available; it never waits at the end of a film.

Presentation Mode removes scroll progression, not user control. The presenter
holds each rest indefinitely, uses Next/Previous or a supported remote signal,
opens a genuine look and returns to the identical held frame. Optional Fullscreen
uses the browser API; unsupported/denied requests keep an in-page presentation.
No automatic fullscreen on a page load, no audio, no timer advances.

Supported while the stage itself is focused: ArrowRight/ArrowDown/PageDown/Space
forward; ArrowLeft/ArrowUp/PageUp/Shift+Space backward; Home/End first/last.
Buttons, links, inputs, menus, dialogs and the photo reader retain their own key
behavior. Escape closes inspection → reader/archive/world selector → presentation,
without swallowing normal site input. Remote vendors differ; visible controls
are the guaranteed fallback. Keyboard focus makes quiet controls visible.

Ordinary Explore scrolls a short local viewing sequence; it does not force
slide snapping or traversal through the other three worlds. Mode exit restores
the previous Explore position. Reduced motion uses direct complete compositions.

## Scope and approval

This is an isolated proposal and real browser proof, not a public replacement.
Phase C authors the Streetwear motion vocabulary and tests the other destinations
as visual/keynote studies. Three unique candidate photos per world are enough to
judge this grammar; **forty sourced display slots and full four-world production
integration are explicitly not complete**. The ten-slot strategy is in CONTENT.md.

STOP after published proof and request artistic approval of the frames, camera
gate, navigator and keynote controls. Source/router integration, forty-look
curation, full history contract and shared-reader extraction require the next
explicit task. Protected application files remain unchanged.
