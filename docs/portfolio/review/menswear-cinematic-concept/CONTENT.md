## Sourcing/replacement acceptance

Select 26 further distinct full-look references only after the concept decision.
Prioritize street volume/layering, genuine performance attire, contemporary
separates and refined tailoring in their actual categories. Verify each asset's
page, creator, reuse terms, dimensions, complete framing and any brand/endorsement
restrictions. Reject watermarks, cropped primary bodies, low resolution, uncertain
rights and the same garment counted as multiple angles.

Replace temporary media later through slot metadata, with owner-supplied rights,
source/creator, accurate alt text, dimensions and measured view geometry. Never
silently inherit an old source's crop or credit, or promote ownership by editing
copy. Original sources remain preserved. Verified project publication needs its
own audit of world-level publication behavior, historical aliases and mixed states.
No Sketch/Pattern/Flat/CLO stages are in this Menswear scope.
