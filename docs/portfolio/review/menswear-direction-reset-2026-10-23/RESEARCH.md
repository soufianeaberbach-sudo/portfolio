## Research inference

Casualwear, Activewear, Outerwear and Tailoring form a clear curated showroom. Retailers often use Jackets & Coats or Suits & Blazers/Tailoring and retain overlapping product filters. Outerwear and tailoring should therefore be separate exhibition groups, while relaxed daily streetwear belongs within Casualwear. This is an editorial merchandising choice awaiting owner confirmation.

A COS home open returned an older Summer 2025 cache despite a current search label; it is excluded as 2026 proof. Older Massimo Dutti linen pages are construction precedents only and are not used to establish current season. No exact branded design, graphic, product photo or campaign identity is replicated.
