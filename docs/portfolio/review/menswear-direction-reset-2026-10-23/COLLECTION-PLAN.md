## Collection logic
Casualwear balances denim, jersey, knitwear, linen and shirting. Activewear separates sporting functions and lifestyle uses. Outerwear spans ten distinct constructions from light shell to long wool coat. Tailoring distinguishes cut, material, construction and occasion, including separates and evening dress. These are curated showroom groups; retailer categories overlap.
Casting is natural, handsome contemporary adult commercial menswear casting, usually 22–35. Similar profiles are acceptable. No eccentric traits are assigned to demonstrate diversity. Model identity remains constant within each later three-view set.
Every fabric composition, weight and seam specification below is a proposed original design target. Images do not prove physical construction, exact composition, performance or manufacture. Market references inform breadth, not copying.
## White studio and production
Independent premium neutral-white seamless studio, broad soft frontal and overhead light, subtle real contact shadow, neutral colour, portrait 2:3. Model including shoes occupies about 86–89% frame height; full head and footwear, comfortable white margins. No props, logos or watermark.
All looks: Front = full-length straight-on natural stance; Back = genuine full rear, preserving model and every outfit detail; Editorial = a separate photograph following the product-specific treatment below. No mirror views. Before companions, review the Front and lock a visible-construction continuity sheet including button counts, pocket count, rear treatment and footwear. Any visual drift requires a new version and truthful rejection record, never overwriting the original.
Exports: 1800 × 2700 per view, later 5400 × 2700 three-view masters. Preserve unmodified native bytes and true dimensions. Uniform resampling only; no stretched figures or garment cropping.
