approaches without losing the complete look. Pink shirt / stone trousers **02**
interrupt the long dress line. The stone jumpsuit **03** replaces satin in the

## 02 — Activewear / the relay

The red colourblocked set **08** establishes the stepping diagonal. A controlled

## 03 — Streetwear / counterweight

## Shared photographic behavior

Every archive contains its category's complete unique set: **17 / 10 / 2 / 11 / 9**.
Every primary cinematic occurrence is front/primary, or the genuine whole image
when inseparable. A reader selection never changes the exhibition's primary view.

The reader consumes `Subject.views` through a small final-photography adapter.
Separable composites provide Front, Back and Complete original. Separate supplied
Front, Back, Side, Editorial and Detail assets use their own images and dimensions.
Unsupported controls do not appear. The current nine Swim images are single-view;
four other sources are inseparable pairs. Unknown geometry falls back to the whole
original. No technical story is rendered publicly, including for future subjects.

Return deliberately resolves on the primary photograph before its measured cut,
so the exhibition never inherits an automatic back view. The existing router
retains hash/history, Escape, scroll lock and focus ownership. Keyboard/reduced
motion settle without forced cinematic playback; no-JS keeps resting compositions,
complete archives and native garment targets.

Some originals retain visible paper/studio texture and existing photographic
artifacts. Occasion and Swim use a restrained photographic edge to make those
source surfaces intentional. No destructive background removal or recolouring is
performed. Source limitations remain inspectable in the complete originals.

## Proposal critique and limits

The first proposals risked the same one-to-pair rhythm across categories. Occasion
was changed to the central-axis opening, Swim to a dominant-centre composition,
and Streetwear shortened. Mobile is edited independently. Motion connects these
resting compositions; it does not certify their artistic approval.

This remains a review candidate. Final desktop and physical iPhone approval is a
human checkpoint; emulation and tests do not substitute for it.
