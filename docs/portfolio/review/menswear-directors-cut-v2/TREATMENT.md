# THE ROOM WITHIN — a proposed Streetwear Director's Cut

## Authority and frozen baseline

V1's interaction foundation is accepted; its creative direction is **not
approved**. This revision starts from `045bedaded6e3b168dafb60b010f613eca209f6c`
on `feat/menswear-directors-cut-v2`. The reviewed branch and all V1 evidence remain
unchanged. Main was independently rechecked at
`65ab3a07c2729b42cd72e50b11ff7f63767c614b` on 19 October 2026.

This is one Streetwear film proposal. The three other destinations retain their
V1 studies for navigation continuity; they are not being developed or approved.

## Director's critique of V1

V1's scene state, source decoding, direct jump, speaker controls and occurrence
return are useful. Its visual logic was replacement: caption announces subject,
photo moves, another photo arrives. The black field did not change what the
audience understood about the image. The teal finale was withheld, but not earned.
The presenter had pauses without a sufficiently interesting passage between them.
Repeating the hoodie established familiarity without developing its meaning.

This revision must change the *reading of photographic space*. One source's
apparently abstract architectural fragment will become the destination itself.
That gives the transition a subject beyond relocating pictures.

## Three treatments considered before replacing the film

### 1. The room within — selected for motion development

**Before:** a complete city stride is held to one side. A pale vertical plane
occupies the other side; it appears to be a limit or architectural obstruction.
It is actually a close window on the empty white booth in source 30599804.

**Expectation:** the city photo will pass behind another graphic panel, or a new
image will enter beside it. **Actual event:** the pale plane crosses the stage,
then the viewing distance opens *within that same source*. Its photographed
white wall acquires a roof, perspective lines, a floor and a complete man in
loose denim. What looked like an abstract obstruction was another photographic
room all along. No simulated 3D, invented angle or figure animation.

The surprise is a change of information, not just a color change. Architectural
constraint is then released: the white wall becomes narrow peripheral wings and
the oversized teal look occupies the opening. The finale returns to the opening's
theme of volume, now an assertive, sculptural silhouette rather than neutral
hooded volume. This is editorial association between independent photographs,
not a claim of one campaign, model, location or design history.

On mobile, the obstruction enters from the right edge and briefly owns the
portrait field before the complete booth view resolves. The final wings leave
up/down or laterally according to available space; the entire teal look remains
inside the stage. In Presentation Mode the same continuous path connects five
indefinitely held compositions. Reduced motion cuts directly to those full looks.

**Risk:** a window on the booth must contain architecture only at the expectation
rest. A chopped model in that window would destroy the premise. The pull-back
must reveal the actual source, not synthesize an architectural set.

### 2. Chorus / silence

A dense, asymmetrical field of three whole street looks builds spatial pressure.
The visitor expects an ensemble finale. Instead a decisive cut removes all three
and one teal figure holds the whole screen. No source registration or common-floor
claim is needed. Mobile alternates a low/full-width photographic axis with a tall
one, then resolves into one upright figure; the keynote can pause on each field.

The surprise comes from subtraction and silence. It serves the clothes because
the final garment no longer competes. Its weakness is also clear in the visual
test: before the cut, the collection still reads as a curated image arrangement.
It risks refining the very gallery grammar the owner rejected.

### 3. A cut in volume

Cream hoodie and teal tailoring occupy matching full-height framing in sequence.
A short field traversal follows the dropped shoulder/outer contour, then a hard
editorial cut changes soft neutral volume into structured color. The camera never
pretends the two bodies register or belong to the same garment. Mobile uses two
complete portrait holds; the presenter can step directly or reverse the cut.

The audience expects another angle or another neutral uniform. It sees a different
interpretation of volume. This is visually economical and would suit a short
campaign bumper. With these unrelated poses, however, the geometric rhyme is
not strong enough to sustain a memorable transformation. Additional registered
owner photography would make it more credible. A software morph would not.

## Visual selection, not technical convenience

[Desktop alternatives](alternatives-desktop.jpg) and
[mobile alternatives](alternatives-mobile.jpg) show actual source compositions,
before and after each proposed surprise. They were rendered before the V2 motion
controller was written. The room-within treatment has a discoverable piece of
real visual information that the other two do not: the first panel and its later
room are the *same photograph*. This makes a continuously watched transition
worth developing. It does not automatically make the final motion successful.

## Directorial argument

1. **Subject beyond clothing:** how an outfit occupies and eventually commands a
   frame. Soft volume → city direction → an enclosing room → sculptural presence.
2. **Dominant emotion:** anticipation generated by a partially understood space,
   rather than excitement generated by motion speed.
3. **First shot:** an entire cream hooded look, held near the central axis. It
   establishes the scale of a body and a restrained interpretation of volume.
4. **Eye direction:** first face/shoulder → trouser fall; then the real city stride
   and the vertical white obstruction; then the reveal of the person beside it.
5. **Withheld information:** the identity and extent of the architectural source,
   followed by the colored oversized final silhouette.
6. **Next shot's change:** the stage gains a direction and a foreground limit,
   not a second equal-sized card or a new slogan.
7. **Expectation:** the barrier appears to close the image field. Its photographed
   seam is an anchor through the movement.
8. **Surprise:** an obstruction becomes a comprehensible room by opening the
   field of view within its actual two-dimensional image.
9. **Meaning:** the clothes are understood against different kinds of enclosure;
   the final oversized volume will no longer be constrained by these wings.
10. **Final revelation:** the opening's neutral volume has become an independent,
    assertive fashion statement. No imaginary costume change is implied.
11. **Apparent camera:** lateral approach to a photographic gate, then a reverse
    establishing movement, finally release into a frontal hero hold.
12. **Source support:** booth source contains real empty wall, seams, roof and a
    complete figure. Crops are reversible windows, not erased backgrounds.
13. **Atmosphere:** quiet paper/concrete → urban density/pale obstruction → luminous
    photographed architecture → restrained peripheral enclosure → ink surrounding
    an intact pale-grey studio portrait. No pixel relighting or garment recolor.
14. **Presenter pauses:** all five act rests retain a complete primary look, clear
    stage margins and quiet orientation. No dramatic action must finish on a timer.
15. **Without animation:** the full hoodie, asymmetrical city/barrier, resolved
    booth, framed teal and final frontal teal must each stand as editorial images.
16. **In motion:** the obstruction-to-room transformation is the actual experiment.
    Its continuity, occlusion, source limits and interruption must be judged in
    the final continuous browser recordings, not inferred from this description.
17. **Film versus slides:** no per-act headline substitution is allowed to carry
    the narrative. The photographic gate and its source coordinates connect the
    passage; visual critique may still reject it if that connection is unconvincing.

The last two acts deliberately keep one subject: first discovered through the
remaining frame, then able to own it. That is a changed spatial relationship, not
the same photo recycled under two headings. If the difference is imperceptible
in the browser it must be revised rather than justified in prose.

## Interaction contract

Preserve V1's native Explore scroll, manual scene choices, Next/Previous, optional
fullscreen, stage-focused remote keys, direct four-world jump, always-available
archive, native reader and exact source/scroll/focus return. No autoplay or
forced delay. Direct scene/world jumps skip the intervening path. Normal
Next/Previous follows the authored path and remains interruptible.

Only complete visible photographic doors are interactive. Architectural windows
are evidence of framing, not new garments, archive entries or invented angles.
The whole source remains available in the reader. Windowed sources must not
pretend to register geometrically with the uncropped original during entry.

No new public router. No changes to V1 evidence, Womenswear, public Menswear,
shared implementation or production configuration. No forty-look sourcing,
multi-angle invention or garment-development stages. Stop at artistic review.
