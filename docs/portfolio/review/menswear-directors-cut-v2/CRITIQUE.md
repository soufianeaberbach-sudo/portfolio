# Director's self-assessment

The owner rejected V1's creative result. The purpose here is to earn a new
artistic decision, not to reverse that verdict through test counts.

## What changed substantively

V1 used titles to announce five arrangements. V2 keeps one quiet opening line
and lets photographic information establish the acts. The booth photograph is
introduced through its empty architecture, then discovered as a complete room.
The same source's disjoint leaves make the final opening. This carries a visual
anchor across the middle rather than inserting a dark-background interlude.

The ending is discovered through that opening and then held without the inherited
frame. It reprises the opening's *idea of volume*, not the earlier photograph.
The denim look never pretends to change into the teal look. All photographs retain
their independent identity and provenance.

The reader needed a local extension because its new camera windows are narrower
than the original image. Uniform same-source scale plus measured clip expansion
preserves continuity without stretching the person or replacing the original.
World jumps, presenter controls, archive and exact return retain the accepted V1
foundation. No shared implementation was altered for this extension.

## Film judgment

The strongest passage is **expectation → room discovery**. What looked like an
enclosing plane acquires context from its own photograph. That is a visual
revelation with a source-supported reason, not another caption/image swap.
The opening can be held as a complete quiet look; the last frame holds a complete
colored, sculptural silhouette with no competing headline. The two final acts
use the same look intentionally: first framed by inherited architecture, then
free of it. Their changing relationship is visible without a new slogan.

This is still an editorial film made from static photographs, not footage of a
person performing or a camera entering a real shared set. The architectural
opening may read as a graphic aperture to some viewers. The owner must judge
whether its photographic continuity and final release reach the desired stage
standard; technical correctness cannot answer that question.

## Remaining limits

- Independent city, booth, concrete and grey-studio sources cannot establish an
  actual single-location campaign. The staging acknowledges their photographic
  edges; it does not synthesize shared floors, light or perspective.
- The central movement briefly travels across a larger photographic crop before
  resolving to a whole look. Its purpose is the architecture-to-room reveal,
  not a claim that a cut-off body is an acceptable primary garment presentation.
- New owner photography with planned wide architectural frames and registered
  full looks could support subtler composition and higher-resolution projection.
  No new camera angle or wardrobe relationship is fabricated to conceal this gap.
- Physical iPhone Safari, presenter remote vendors, real projector contrast and
  sustained frame pacing remain unverified. No 60fps or 4K-quality promise.
- Other worlds remain unchanged V1 studies, not silently upgraded or approved.
  Forty-look curation and public integration are outside this cut.

The result is submitted for artistic review. Approval is not declared here.
