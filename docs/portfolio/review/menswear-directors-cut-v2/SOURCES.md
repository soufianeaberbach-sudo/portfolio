## Non-destructive camera windows

Coordinates are normalized to the actual unmodified source, not body masks:

- Cream and city primary rests: complete supplied photographs.
- Booth desktop rest: `[0, .20, 1, .80]`; only empty upper architecture is trimmed.
- Booth portrait rest: `[.46, .25, .54, .75]`; architecture narrows to leave the
  complete person, hands and shoes readable. The full original remains in inspection.
- Teal rest: `[.24, 0, .60, 1]`; trims empty grey side margins only, preserving
  full hat, shoulder/jacket width, hems and footwear.
- The booth's two leaves split at source x=.50, to the left of the model. They
  show disjoint regions, not duplicate garments. Their peripheral held positions
  retain architecture only; the complete person leaves through a directed
  photographic occlusion, not a destructive background removal.

During the brief barrier/room transition the viewport travels across the real
photograph. It can temporarily occlude parts of the image; this is not a primary
full-look rest or an invented garment detail. Reduced motion omits this travel.
Every designed primary rest shows the complete available look. No source pixels
are warped, rotated into fake perspective, recolored, relit or fabricated.

The reader uses the complete original and its real native proportions. A measured
same-source clone expands the actual display crop through clip travel and uniform
scale. It never stretches a narrow crop into a wide body. The return reverses
that registration; resizing recomputes layout rather than retaining old pixels.

These are single supplied photographs. **PRIMARY PHOTOGRAPH** is truthful; no
Back, Side, alternate editorial angle or technical-development stage is invented.
Current downloaded source resolution is documented; a final 4K/projector delivery
specification requires owner photography and separate verification.
