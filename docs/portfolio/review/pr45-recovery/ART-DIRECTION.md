# Independent Menswear review and unapproved proposal

22 October 2026. Actual Portfolio operated in Chromium at all six specified sizes,
plus 1920×1080 Presentation Mode. Previous recordings were inspected, then fresh
browser captures and continuous motion were made. This is a critique and proposal;
**none of the proposed choreography below is implemented**. The review HTML's
labels/controls explain the study; they are not a proposed replacement UI.

The current product has dependable full-look holds and direct control. It still
relies on unrelated photographic plates and hard replacements in several passages.
The room reveal supplies a genuine spatial idea; no other movement should be
credited with that continuity simply because it animates. Temporary sources from
independent shoots cannot become a coherent moving-camera campaign by masking.

## Streetwear — preserve discovery; refine the last relationship

**Keep:** cream opening, actual room architecture/seam, complete teal and quilted
silhouettes, concise type, direct navigation. The original photograph genuinely
explains the preceding architectural obstruction. That remains the strongest idea.

**Observed weakness:** the last open/compact comparison has unequal photographic
weight and disappears into a full grey stage. On portrait it becomes a teal-to-
puffer replacement plus a global ground change. [Current phone payoff](screens/m-streetwear-2-390.jpg)
and the first row of [decoded desktop frames](current-transition-frames-1440.jpg)
show it. The room's discovery does not itself explain that grey atmosphere.

**Proposal:** retain the room sequence. Show the complete open and compact forms
at equal *visible* scale, aligning the source windows on one stage baseline. Hold
the comparison briefly, then cut to the complete quilted look on the white stage;
its real grey photographic ground remains a deliberate plate. No recolouring,
background removal or wipe through the body. On portrait, use the two full looks
sequentially at a stable scale/axis; do not shrink them into a duet.

[Desktop storyboard](storyboard-streetwear-1440.jpg) · [phone storyboard](storyboard-streetwear-390.jpg).
**Worth doing, approval required.** Risk: source-qualified baselines must fall back
to complete-photo fit when owner replacements arrive. No extra decoded images or
longer scroll are needed. It remains an independent-photo comparison, not one room.

## Activewear — let the pose create the release

**Keep:** genuine low stance, strong colour contrast, complete raised-knee payoff,
restrained controls. The photographs contain real posture and directional contrast.

**Observed weakness:** the diagonal wipe temporarily combines fragments of the
white standing photograph and the raised-knee image. It reads as an interface
mask rather than a change in athletic tension. See the second row of the
[current decoded frames](current-transition-frames-390.jpg). The standing plate
before it supplies little compression, so the mask carries too much of the story.

**Proposal:** move the existing low pose to the final preparation, then make one
clean cut to the actual raised-knee pose. Keep red/white earlier for rhythm; do
not repeat the low pose as a redundant new scene. Match attention through centre
and direction, not body outlines. No fabricated leap, common athlete or performance
claim. Portrait centres the complete horizontal stance as a short transitional
composition, then resolves to the tall complete figure; no extra blank scroll or
small side-by-side comparison. The wide pose remains constrained by its real ratio.

[Desktop storyboard](storyboard-activewear-1440.jpg) · [phone storyboard](storyboard-activewear-390.jpg).
**Highest-priority proposal, approval required.** Reordering the cinematic roles
requires revising scene labels and seek positions together; archive identities and
counts stay unchanged. Existing decoding/interruptibility remains sufficient.

## Casualwear — reduce the number of ideas

**Keep:** complete seated and carried-chair photographs, support/posture comparison,
cream overshirt ending, quiet neutral palette. These have a visible editorial link.

**Observed weakness:** the green shoulder-cloth insert interrupts that relationship,
then the overshirt arrives as another replacement. The third row of
[decoded desktop frames](current-transition-frames-1440.jpg) and
[current phone ending](screens/m-rtw-2-390.jpg) show a complete but weakly earned finale.
The garments are legible; the edit's emphasis is diffuse.

**Proposal:** retain seated → carried support; omit the green look from the film
(it stays in the ten-look archive). Cut directly from the carried structure to the
quiet overshirt and hold. On wide screens leave looking room toward the face's
orientation; portrait keeps the complete photograph centred. No prop morph,
photographic overlap or extra headline. A hold is one held composition, not a
second duplicate scene with a new slogan.

[Desktop storyboard](storyboard-casualwear-1440.jpg) · [phone storyboard](storyboard-casualwear-390.jpg).
**Worth doing, approval required.** Risk: fewer scenes require deliberate mapping
of manual controls and Explore positions, not stretching remaining shots to fill
the former duration. No content deletion or reader change is proposed.

## Tailoring & Outerwear — retain the honest context cut

**Keep:** dark/ivory reversal, calm structure, complete figures, dark ground only
where the photograph supports it, and the intact outerwear/location finale.
[Current wide payoff](screens/m-tailoring-2-1440.jpg) is a strong pause frame.

**Observed limit:** the studio-to-street transition is clearly a cut between
independent shoots. Source contrast/scale changes are visible, especially on phone.
However, the actual doorway/context adds meaning that a decorative wipe would
remove. It does not demonstrate a continuous moving camera.

**Recommendation:** retain its staging and clean cut. Do not invent a spatial
bridge or add masking merely to make every world change. Hold the ivory suit,
then the full coat/context. The storyboards describe retention, **not a new design**.

[Desktop retention board](storyboard-tailoring-1440.jpg) · [phone retention board](storyboard-tailoring-390.jpg).
**No substantial visual revision recommended.** Possible future source-matched
photography could strengthen continuity; no inferred tailoring construction is claimed.

## Cross-world findings and boundaries

- Full primary silhouettes are readable across six sizes. Typography is secondary
  after entry; the 1024px Casualwear clearance fix remains useful. Preserve it.
- Source rectangles and differing studio grounds are still visible. Some are
  intentional plates; they are not seamless campaign photography. Never erase
  pale garments or recolour a source to imply continuity.
- Portrait clean cuts protect full figures but currently make three worlds feel
  similar. Change the *reason and order* of the cuts before adding movement.
- Archive, reader, inspection and return screenshots show access stays clear.
  No fifth Womenswear world or mandatory Menswear activation is proposed.
- Source files, colours, bodies and proportions remain intact in all studies.
  Existing measured empty-margin windows are reused; no new anatomy crop.
- The proposal retains Archivo/Inter/Mono and established white/ink/blue language.
  No new framework, global palette, UI system or development narrative.

before any substantial production visual edit. Technical fixes are independently
implemented and verified. Artistic approval is not implied by those tests.
