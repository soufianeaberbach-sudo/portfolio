# Five edits — Womenswear V2.1

One photographic stage and one reader, five directed edits. The complete primary
silhouette is the subject. No automatic back/side/editorial change occurs.
The timeline uses semantic garment roles, not archive rank or odd/even positions.
Each archive now contains ten distinct garments, including looks outside the cast.

## 01 — Ready-to-Wear: line / interruption

Champagne satin (`01`) opens beside the existing title. Approach establishes its
complete length. Pink shirting (`02`) interrupts the long line. The stone jumpsuit
(`03`) restores it; printed shirting and rust trousers (`05`) give the final shot
colour. Desktop uses separated diagonal exchanges; phone preserves one dominant
complete look at a time. The existing composition and pace are retained.
Ten-look curation keeps satin, shirting, jumpsuit, diagonal stripes, printed
separates, rust mini, olive maxi, white lace trousers, black lace separates and
green knit. Excluded seven keep their original readers and URLs.
Archive → Activewear through the existing category handoff.

## 02 — Activewear: precision / release

Red colourblocking (`08`) establishes direction. Black racerback (`04`) takes the
relay before the white/red-striped set (`01`) provides the midpoint contrast.
The final movement now opens the leg into the brown ribbed flare (`07`). On
desktop the white stripe and brown flare form a separated, complete pair; phone
releases the white look upward and resolves on the full brown silhouette.
This changes the garment vocabulary, rather than repeating the white shot under
a new label. Shorter, more decisive movement than Occasion; no invented athletic
motion. Ten-look archive → Streetwear.

## 03 — Streetwear: planted / lifted / grounded

The existing burgundy cropped hoodie (`02`) anchors the opening. A brown oversized
hoodie and lifted knee (`03`) answer its planted stance. The juxtaposition keeps
the two photographic grounds honest and the bodies separate. Olive shirting and
wide white trousers (`05`) move the eye into a longer, relaxed volume. Black
utility separates with extended sleeves (`04`) conclude with width and weight.
Four selected looks, not ten animated thumbnails. All ten appear in the archive.
Phone uses full-look exchanges; studio-margin windows only remove measured empty
lateral space. Wider originals balance vertically instead of falling below a
blank upper screen. The choreography replaces the former two-look counterweight;
it is not its old geometry stretched to fill a longer track.
Archive → the slower Occasion chapter.

## 04 — Occasion: ceremony / fall

Preserve the black sequinned column (`04`), champagne fishtail (`11`) and final
navy lace silhouette (`03`). The camera gives each hem room. Desktop opens the
central axis after the opposing long silhouettes; phone uses slower vertical
exchanges and complete views. Ten selected references retain the original IDs;
the redundant dark-brown column (`09`) remains directly enterable.
Authentic paper/studio texture and original photographic artefacts remain intact.
A fine source edge declares that surface; no background or fabric is recoloured.
Archive → Swim.

## 05 — Swim: clarity / last light

Blue ring-front one-piece (`06`) establishes the opening. Black bandeau (`08`)
creates the graphic midpoint. Silver halter (`05`) briefly cools the palette.
The final vertical exchange now reveals the white cut-out one-piece (`10`) on
its authentic muted-pink studio ground. It changes shape, stance, colour and
photographic atmosphere; the silver frame no longer doubles as the phone payoff.
No unrelated bodies are overlapped to fabricate a scene. Single-view sources
stay single-view. Phone gives each complete primary representation its own clear
focus, then opens the ten-look archive. Final handoff remains Menswear.
