The canonical machine-readable provenance is
[`src/data/womenswear-references.json`](../../../../src/data/womenswear-references.json).
It records page and direct retrieval URLs, license, creator, original page
pixel dimensions, retrieved dimensions/hash, local responsive dimensions,
modifications and replacement status for every addition. File-format conversion
and proportional downsampling are the only pixel modifications. No retouching,
background removal, recolouring, generative imagery or fabricated alternate view.
The 900/1800-high WebP renditions serve different responsive sizes; downloaded
JPEG intermediates and HTML research pages are not committed.

Source-qualified presentation windows on four selected new photographs remove
only measured empty lateral studio margins. They do not cut a garment, head,
hand or foot and do not alter the source files. Look Closer links the complete
local source. Other new photographs use the whole original framing. Their
single primary views do not generate Back/Side buttons.

The eight Streetwear additions are eight distinct outfits, not eight poses of
one outfit. Cropped candidate 6311663, portrait 9514687, partial denim 12093815,
and the alternative pose 19132420 were rejected. Candidate 8423863 was less
appropriate to the intended street styling. No candidate was duplicated under
another garment ID.

Historical 49 source files and data records remain unchanged. Their historical
metadata does not name external photographers; the manifest states that missing
provenance explicitly. This task does not retrospectively claim a license or
owner authorship for those existing assets. All remain temporary references.
