## Defects found and corrected

1. **Closed full-reference disclosure:** a closed `details` descendant could still
   report dimensions. Treating it as a return door dropped focus to body. Hidden
   disclosure contents are excluded from source discovery; cold historical links
   return to the visible world control, opened disclosures return to the exact link.
2. **Archive Arrow Right:** the inner archive handled the key, then the enclosing
   film handler advanced to Activewear. A real before run recorded that wrong hash.
   The controller now respects `defaultPrevented` and interactive archive/summary
   targets. The same desktop/phone reproduction passes without a world/vertical jump.
3. **Escape and passive orientation:** returning to bare `#menswear` could immediately
   reapply the first category hash and consume repeated Escape. Passive orientation
   now preserves that legitimate root state until progression changes world.
4. **Native fragment focus:** browser Back to a real chapter anchor applied native
   focus after popstate. The router reconciles source focus after that phase, guarded
   against a newer world, reader or deliberate focus. Bare chapter Back/Forward now
   preserves both scroll and the actual occurrence in both chapters.
5. **Presentation Home:** an initial broader run timed out waiting for animated Home.
   A separate six-size controlled investigation did not reproduce it; its original
   cause is **not established or labelled environmental**. Home/End were changed to
   immediate endpoint seeks, matching direct-navigation semantics. The replaced
   animated endpoint path is covered by 264 endpoint/interruption/focus checks and
   the 585-assertion production suite. Assertions were retained, not relaxed.
6. **Visual first pass:** simultaneous portrait volume plates overlapped; a Tailoring
   shutter combined pieces of two unrelated bodies. Both were rejected during review.
   Portrait now uses intact sequential photographs. Activewear's last reveal first
   isolates the white plate, avoiding a third competing figure underneath the cut.

The previous measured clone-origin, cold arrival, height clamping, primary-only
views, image-failure feedback, touch targets, accurate captions and source credits
remain. Actual before/after evidence is linked from the review index.

7. **Compact-desktop title clearance:** the 1024px Casualwear opening left almost
   no separation between “Evolution” and the photograph. A scoped 1000–1199px
   title scale restores breathing room; the same actual browser case was recaptured.

8. **Manual motion timing:** decoded video revealed that controller easing was
   compounding the renderer’s authored easing and rushing central two-shot holds.
   The controller now advances linearly; the film renderer retains its intentional
   shot easing and holds. Durations were not lengthened. The affected desktop/phone
   transition recordings were regenerated and reviewed from actual decoded frames.

9. **Direct garment replacement:** changing a Womenswear garment URL while another
   reader was open could borrow the prior clicked source for its entry cut. Source
   identity validation now applies to both photographic readers. The reproduced
   case checks the real destination photograph, absence of a cross-subject clone
   and correct destination return on desktop/phone.
