# Portfolio Visual Acceptance Contract

## 1. Intent
Create one continuous, native-scroll journey in which real fashion work attracts, real construction evidence proves capability, and technology amplifies the path from idea to product: Opening → Womenswear → Menswear → Tech Packs → Pattern Development → Ending.

## 2. Non-negotiable outcomes
- The real four worlds are the journey; there is no duplicate preview layer.
- The Opening is master-scene ready, responsive, subject-aware, and replaceable through content configuration.
- Every chapter has a distinct personality within Bone / Paper / Ink, with accessible accent usage.
- Each boundary semantically transforms the outgoing world into the next without crossfade, snap scrolling, or scroll-speed hijacking.
- Existing viewers, readers, content provenance, keyboard behavior, native scrolling, progressive enhancement, and routes remain intact.
- Pattern Development is the intellectual climax; the Ending resolves quietly to Bone / Paper / Ink.

## 3. Creative freedom
Composition, shared geometry, registration systems, crop behavior, masks, typography, and restrained scroll-linked motion may change when they clarify the narrative. No new framework or fabricated work/evidence is permitted.

## 4. Failure conditions
Failure includes unsafe face/garment crops; duplicated content; unreadable foregrounds; Amber/Violet used as inaccessible small text; static-divider-only or fading transitions; dark/sci-fi Pattern Development; truncated index labels or sub-44px touch targets; dramatic dark Ending; ambiguous reference/demo provenance; background focus behind a viewer; unintended overflow; obsolete tests; or screenshots captured but not inspected.

## 5. Required visual evidence
Fresh review evidence must include 11 desktop and 6 mobile states covering the Opening, every active world, every transition midpoint, and Ending. Each image must be opened and reviewed for crop, garment visibility, collisions, contrast, hierarchy, spacing, balance, color, index continuity, transition meaning, double exposure, mobile composition, overflow, and overall intentionality. Engineering checks and visual acceptance are separate gates; passing tests never constitutes design approval.
