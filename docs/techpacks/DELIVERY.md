## Integration and checks

The five records retain their original type/order. Generated URLs/page counts/previews are synchronized in `src/data/techpacks.json`, consumed by `src/data/portfolio.ts`. `TechPackWorld.astro` keeps the existing document-first list, chapter navigation and dialog reader, adds direct Open/Download links, and replaces demo/production-ready copy with sample-validation wording. Original demo assets and portfolio photographs are preserved. No other portfolio chapter is reworked.

Executed successfully: Astro build; five document integrity/engineering validations; six mutation tests; 303 smoke checks; 108 source fingerprints; 40 navigation checks; 20 chapter-history checks; and all five document/preview/download/keyboard-reader cycles at 1440, 1366, 1024, 768, 430 and 390 widths in pinned Chromium. Browser evidence reports zero page errors or horizontal overflow. This is Chromium testing, including mobile emulation; physical Safari was not tested.

No physical sample, approved pattern, fabric tests, supplier approval, colour standard, care/origin verification or marker was available. These remain production-release requirements. All numeric development values are proposed; confirmed means supported by the cited visual source, not physically verified. No merge or production deployment is included.
