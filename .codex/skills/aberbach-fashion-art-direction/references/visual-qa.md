# Visual QA

Use this after a coherent pass. These are judgment prompts, not rigid geometry
assertions or instructions to redesign protected areas.

## Seven critique questions

1. **Fashion hierarchy:** Is the garment/work immediately readable and the hero?
2. **Image integration:** Are grounds intentional, without accidental rectangles,
   halos, mismatched neutrals or awkward crops?
3. **Composition:** Is scale intentional, whitespace useful and the focal point
   clear? Can important construction and silhouette details be read?
4. **Rhythm:** Does the whole journey build, pause and vary while remaining
   coherent, with clear chapter orientation and transitions?
5. **Typography:** Does text orient and explain rather than compete with fashion?
6. **Interaction:** Does motion reveal content, support orientation or give useful
   feedback? Check smoothness, interruption and reduced-motion access.
7. **Mobile:** Are scale, crop, order and pacing deliberately edited? Check touch,
   focus, scroll, viewport safety and zoom access, not just desktop stacking.

## Failure conditions

- Accidental white rectangles around studio photography, halos or ground seams.
- Garments too small while the layout dominates.
- Decorative dead space or mobile journeys padded with decorative screens.
- Generic card/grid treatment without a content-driven reason.
- Category differences expressed mainly as gimmicks or separate UI engines.
- Arbitrary image scattering unsupported by real content relationships.
- Crops that cut important garment information.
- Labels or metadata more visible than the fashion.
- Excessive pinned scrolling or motion that advertises the interface.
- Weak transitions between chapters or unrelated-microsite rhythm.
- Green tests while the visible result is obviously poor.
- A UI concept requiring code comments to explain why the composition exists.

## Positive checks

- Garments readable immediately, with intentional scale hierarchy.
- Coherent page ground and intentional studio-background integration/framing.
- Visual relationships supported by real project content and evidence.
- Controlled whitespace that supports a clear focal point.
- Clean chapter orientation and strong full-journey rhythm.
- Typography and restrained motion helping access and understanding.
- Mobile clarity, useful pacing and preserved evidence access.

Record what was actually seen, fix visible failures and inspect the result again.
Do not repair image-ground problems by destructively editing photography without
explicit user approval.
